# CriterionLens

CriterionLens is a research prototype for matching clinical-trial eligibility
criteria to evidence in a patient note. Its output is preliminary and is not
medical advice or a clinical eligibility decision. A qualified human must
review all source records.

Do not submit real or identifiable patient information. Use synthetic or
appropriately de-identified examples only.
